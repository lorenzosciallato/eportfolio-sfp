# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML (CSS and JS inline), publishable on GitHub Pages. No server in v1.

## Users
Students of Scienze della Formazione Primaria (LM-85bis) at UniMC, all years. They compile the personal profile (usually first year) and reread it in later years to see how their words, resources and questions changed. Mostly on phones, often in short sessions.

## Product Purpose
Replace the current university e-portfolio (previously Mahara, now an in-house page with poor UX and unreliable PDF export) with a guided, step-by-step experience for the "Profilo personale" module that ends in a PDF that actually downloads. Success: a student finishes the profile without friction and gets a clean A4 PDF to submit.

## Positioning
The same official questions of the SFP "Profilo personale", but one at a time, with real guidance, autosave and a PDF built for print rather than a screenshot of a web form.

## Operating Context
The profile is a reflective document submitted within the course (tirocinio / e-portfolio). Content is personal and sometimes delicate (fragilities, difficult experiences). The PDF is the deliverable teachers receive.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- v1 scope: only the "Profilo personale" module — 7 sections, as in the course template: 1) In primo piano (photo, name, year, short intro, five words, something not beautiful but important, three significant experiences, optional image, a quotation + why, life lessons from a person / an error / a difficult experience / school, super-resource); 2) My story and the choice of teaching (+ optional image); 3) The school I carry with me (+ optional image); 4) The teacher I imagine today; 5) Resources I bring; 6) What I still have to learn; 7) The questions I start with.
- The personal photo is the only required field; all else optional.
- Question wording comes from the official template and must be preserved.
- Data stays in the browser in v1 (autosave). Future: cross-device save via access keys (UMS-style) or institutional email, the latter only with approval from responsible teachers — undecided.
- Out of scope for v1: U.D.A. generator, daily lesson, logbook, image feed (from the earlier EduPortfolio prototype).

## Evidence on Hand
- Current official template: the "Profilo personale – E-Portfolio SFP" HTML shared by the user (source of questions and hints).
- Earlier prototype EduPortfolio (Gemini) with Instagram-like profile header — reference for spirit only.
- No testimonials, logos or official UniMC branding approval; do not imply this is an official UniMC product.

## Product Principles
1. One question at a time; never a wall of text.
2. The PDF must work on every device, first try.
3. The student's own words and photos are the content; the interface recedes.
4. Nothing is lost: autosave always, explicit feedback.
5. Respect for delicate content: private by default, no data leaves the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable on small phones, keyboard-usable, sufficient contrast, respects reduced motion.
