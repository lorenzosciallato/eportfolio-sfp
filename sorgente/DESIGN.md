# Design — Mostra personale

## World
The profile is the student's personal exhibition, seen at night: a dark museum where the archive photographs (public-domain school and gallery images) hang in black and white, and the student's own photos are the only works in colour. Each of the 7 sections is a numbered room (Sala I–VII). The PDF is the printed exhibition catalogue, on warm paper.

Interaction grammar borrowed from music apps: big cover image per room fading into the wall, a horizontal shelf of rooms on the entrance, a persistent bottom player bar (room, question n of N, previous / next) with a progress line.

## Tokens
- wall #100f0d, wall-raise #1a1815, wall-line #2d2a25
- ink #f2ece2, ink-soft #b9b0a3, ink-faint #8f877c
- ottone (brass, museum plaques) #c9a45c, ottone-ink #dcbd7d
- paper (PDF) #f7f3ec, paper-ink #1d1b18
- Display: Bodoni Moda (Italian, Parma, Giambattista Bodoni) 400 / italic / 600
- UI and writing: Hanken Grotesk 400 / 500 / 600

## Signature
- The student's portrait hangs framed (passe-partout) on the entrance gallery wall; empty frame before upload.
- Entering a room: the cover "lights on" (brightness + slight scale), the one authored motion.
- Every archive image carries a museum wall label (didascalia) with source and date.

## Rules
- One question per screen. Official question wording preserved.
- No eyebrows, no gradient text, no card grids of icons.
- Archive images grayscale; user images full colour.
